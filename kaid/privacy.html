KaiD privacy policy

KaiD is a private app with one user, its owner. Only the owner's own Google accounts are connected to it.

Gmail data

With the owner's consent, KaiD reads messages in the connected mailboxes, sorts and summarises them, changes labels, and creates drafts. It sends or deletes a message only after the owner confirms.

Message data is stored on the owner's private server, which is not reachable from the public internet.

Who else receives data

  • To sort and summarise email, message text is sent to AI model providers through OpenRouter.
  • Summaries of important messages are delivered to the owner's private Telegram group.

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect a mailbox in the app or revoke access in their Google account at any time.
